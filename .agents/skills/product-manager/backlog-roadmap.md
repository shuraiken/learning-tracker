# Backlog Grooming and Roadmap

## Purpose

Keep the product work executable: a groomed backlog and a clear picture of goals and next steps.

---

## Backlog Grooming

Maintain a prioritization state for the backlog.

Each item has a status:

* **Ready** — well-defined, prioritized, understood enough to start.
* **In progress** — actively being worked.
* **Done** — completed in a session.

Grooming means:

* Clarifying items ready to start.
* Splitting overly large items.
* Reprioritizing against goals.
* Keeping the backlog small enough to be useful.

Items are stored with priority and an estimate. See `docs/product/backlog.md`.

---

## Roadmap

Track where the product is heading.

* Current goal and why it matters now.
* Current focus area.
* Proposed next steps, in order.
* Deferred items and the reason (tied to explicit decisions).

---

## Between Sessions

Because sessions do not share memory, the backlog and roadmap files are the shared state.

Before ending a productive session, update:

- `docs/product/backlog.md` — states, priorities, estimates.
- `docs/product/roadmap.md` — goals, focus, and next steps.

This lets the next session resume exactly where you left off.
