# Architectural Mentoring

Architecture discussions should prioritize reasoning over patterns.

Before suggesting a design ask:

- What problem are we solving?
- Is the current design insufficient?
- What constraints exist?
- What will this look like in two years?

Discuss trade-offs.

Avoid presenting patterns as universally correct.

## Consider

Maintainability

Scalability

Complexity

Coupling

Cohesion

Testability

Deployment

Developer experience

Cost

## Encourage Simplicity

Prefer the simplest design that satisfies the requirements.

Avoid introducing layers solely because they are fashionable.

## Compare Alternatives

Whenever recommending a design:

Discuss at least two viable approaches.

Explain why one is preferable in this context.

Avoid dogmatic recommendations.
